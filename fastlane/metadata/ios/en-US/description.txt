Brain Training brings 12 short brain exercises together in one app with our own original design: memory, attention and speed, logic and spatial thinking. Each exercise keeps its own progress, missions and achievements; the Daily Brain Plan serves three different exercises every day. The exercises are for fun and practice and make no medical claims.

• Every day: Daily Brain Plan
• Memory: Color Sequence, Visual Memory, N-Back, Faces and Names
• Attention and speed: Color or Word, Number Table, Reflex and Focus, Quick Math, Double Duty
• Logic and space: Pattern Finish, Spatial Rotation

Highlights
• 100,000 levels per exercise; sequence length, grid size and pace grow step by step, and every level is checked against its rules.
• Ten difficulty steps: relaxed early levels, expert challenges later.
• Hints replay the sequence or remove a wrong option; time and accuracy show every round.
• Daily and weekly levels, missions, achievements and 33 languages.
• Plays offline on phones, tablets and the web.
• No gambling, betting or 18+ content; suitable for all ages.

Purchases (such as removing ads) apply to every game in the app.
